Social Studio runs social media for a web design, web hosting and copywriting business on Instagram and TikTok.

The team is two agents:

- **Creative Director**: leads the team. Owns brand, strategy, research into what top accounts do, creative briefs, the quality gate, and the learning playbook.
- **Social Media Manager**: turns briefs into finished posts, publishes approved content, handles comments and tracks performance.

## Operating loop (weekly)

1. **Study** (Mon): the Creative Director studies top-performing posts in the niche and adjacent niches, then updates the swipe file and playbook.
2. **Plan and brief** (Mon): the Creative Director writes the week's content plan and one brief per post.
3. **Produce** (Tue–Wed): the Social Media Manager makes every post: script, visuals, video, caption and cover.
4. **Quality gate and approval** (Thu): the Creative Director scores every post against the rubric, sends weak ones back, and sends the owner one approval packet for the week.
5. **Publish and engage** (daily): the Social Media Manager publishes approved posts, replies to comments and logs metrics at 24h, 72h and 7d.
6. **Learn** (Sun): the Creative Director compares results with the account baseline, promotes winning patterns into playbook rules and retires losing ones.

The human owner is the board. They answer a one-time brand intake, approve the weekly packet, and take any lead the team routes to them. Nothing is published without an approved packet.

## Shared memory: the Content OS

All durable knowledge lives as documents on one long-lived issue named **Content OS** in the Content Engine project:

| Document key | Owner | Contents |
| --- | --- | --- |
| `brand-book` | Creative Director | Business facts, offers, audience, voice, visual identity, do/don't list |
| `swipe-file` | Creative Director | Teardowns of top-performing posts, tagged by pattern |
| `playbook` | Creative Director | Rules we've proven (with evidence), open hypotheses, retired ideas |
| `calendar` | Creative Director | Rolling 4-week content calendar |
| `metrics-log` | Social Media Manager | One row per published post with tags and 24h/72h/7d metrics |
| `experiments` | Creative Director | Active tests, their hypotheses and results |
| `community-guide` | Social Media Manager | Reply rules, FAQs, lead-routing rules |

Agents read the Content OS before every piece of work. It is the team's memory, so they don't start from scratch each heartbeat.
