Set up the team's memory and learn the business. Do this before any content is produced.

1. Create a long-lived issue titled **Content OS** in this project, assigned to yourself and left in `backlog`. Create the documents `brand-book`, `swipe-file`, `playbook`, `calendar`, `metrics-log`, `experiments` and `community-guide` on it, each with its section headings (see COMPANY.md and the skills).
2. Research the business yourself first: its website, any existing social profiles and public reviews. Pre-fill everything you can in `brand-book`, marking each item `verified` or `assumed`.
3. Send the board **one** consolidated intake as a `request_confirmation` or comment, so they can answer in a single sitting. Pre-fill your best guesses so they only need to correct them:
   - Business name, handles, website, location / service area
   - Services and packages (web design, hosting, copywriting), price ranges you may mention publicly, and current availability
   - Ideal client (industry, size, budget) and who you *don't* want
   - Proof you may use publicly: case studies, results, testimonials, portfolio sites, and which clients may be named
   - Voice: 3 adjectives, words to use and avoid, how bold or funny we can be, emoji yes/no
   - Visual identity: logo, colors, fonts, and any existing templates
   - 5–10 accounts they admire and 3–5 competitors
   - Main CTA: book a call, free audit, DM, or link in bio
   - Will they film occasional talking-head clips (yes/no, how often)?
   - Hard no's: topics, claims or styles to avoid
4. When answers arrive, finalize `brand-book` and mark this issue done. Then start the **Content OS baseline** task.
