Build the starting knowledge base so week 1 is based on evidence, not guesses. Start once the brand book is finalized (you can start the research part in parallel with the intake).

1. Build the **watchlist** (25–40 handles across the three rings in the `content-research` skill), including the owner's admired accounts and competitors.
2. Run a first **deep study**: at least 30 teardowns in the `swipe-file`, from at least 15 accounts, using outlier scoring.
3. Write `playbook` v0: 8–12 **hypotheses** (not rules; we have no data yet), each linked to swipe-file evidence.
4. Confirm or adjust the pillar mix and cadence in the Creative Director instructions to fit this brand. Record the final version in `playbook`.
5. Draft a 4-week `calendar` using the 70/20/10 rule (in weeks 1–4, "proven" means strongest research evidence).
6. Design 2–3 **recurring series** (e.g. "Website Roast Wednesday", "One-Line Copy Fix"). Series build habit and make production faster.
7. Hand week 1's briefs to the Social Media Manager and mark this issue done.
